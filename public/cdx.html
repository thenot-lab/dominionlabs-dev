Pre-launch

CDX is a compute exchange for the in-between.

A two-sided marketplace where idle GPU on one operator's machine becomes inference fuel for another's. Cryptographically provenanced. Western-origin model supply chain only. Built on the same audit-chain primitives that run the rest of Dominion Labs.

What it is

CDX — Circuit Dominion Exchange — is the marketplace layer for circular AI compute deals. Compute resources flow between parties: idle capacity from one becomes inference or training fuel for another, with CDX as the matching, execution, and audit layer between them.

This isn't a generic contractor marketplace. It's specifically for AI-compute transactions where supply-chain origin, mTLS-attested execution, and signed delivery receipts matter to both sides.

Status as of 2026-05-20: CDX v2.0.0 repo lives at OneDrive/source/repos/CDX P1/. Hetzner deploy plan staged. Pre-CDX SOC 2 scaffold authored (6 control docs, CC1-CC9 mapped). DNS at cdx.dominionlabs.dev not yet pointed.

Who it's for

Compute suppliers

Operators with idle GPU cycles, mid-sized cloud regions with overcapacity, model-as-a-service operators with off-peak inference headroom.

Compute consumers

Training-run originators, inference-burst customers, region-specific compute needs (Bill 194-aware Ontario procurement, sovereign-origin requirements).

Why circular

Compute resources don't sit idle uniformly. One operator's overcapacity at 3am is another operator's peak-demand window at noon. CDX is the matching layer that makes the in-between profitable for both sides, without requiring either to migrate to a hyperscaler.

What ships with v1

  • Two-sided listing surface: suppliers post capacity, consumers post jobs
  • Matching engine with origin-attestation filtering (Western supply chain only by default)
  • mTLS-attested execution: jobs verify their runtime environment cryptographically
  • Signed delivery receipts via Ed25519 — same primitive used across CSRE / CSCE
  • 2% all-in transaction fee (vs 4-15% mean for compute brokers)
  • Audit log per transaction, hash-chained, queryable by both parties
  • Optional pre-execution origin attestation bundle for Ontario Bill 194 procurement

What it's NOT

  • Not a generic contractor/services marketplace
  • Not a hyperscaler reseller
  • Not for compute sourced from authoritarian-regime suppliers
  • Not running on surveillance / telemetry / behavioral-tracking primitives

Why now

Ontario Bill 194 is tightening AI vendor-eligibility requirements through 2026-2027. Sovereign-origin compute is becoming a real procurement filter, not just a values-aligned preference. CDX is the marketplace layer that makes that filter actionable.

Pricing context: typical compute broker takes are 4-15%. CDX at 2% is well below mean, intentionally. The point is liquidity bootstrapping, not extraction.
