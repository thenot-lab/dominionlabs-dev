★ privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 2026-05-19 · Plain talk version

Short version: we don't track you, we don't sell anything, and we collect only what we need to reply.

Bottom line: if you visit dominionlabs.dev, no analytics or trackers run. If you send a form, we get what you typed and nothing else. If you donate, the payment processor (Stripe or PayPal) handles your card data — we never see it.

What we collect

  • Form submissions. When you use the Talk or Donate forms, your mail client opens with your typed content. We receive whatever you choose to send. That's it.
  • Email correspondence. If you email us, we keep the thread (Gmail handles storage).
  • Payment data. Donations go through Stripe or PayPal. Their privacy policies apply. We see the donation amount + the name/email you provide; we never see your card or bank details.
  • GitHub interactions. If you star/fork/issue/PR on github.com/thenot-lab, GitHub's privacy policy applies.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom, no anything.
  • No cookies (the site doesn't set any).
  • No third-party scripts beyond Google Fonts (CSS only).
  • No tracking pixels in emails.
  • No A/B testing.
  • No marketing automation.

Embedded tools (miniapps)

The Play and Tools sections embed miniapps from miniapps.ai. When you interact with an embedded tool, you are using miniapps.ai directly inside an iframe — their privacy policy applies for whatever the tool does. The iframes are sandboxed.

Custom engagements

If you hire us for consulting, audits, or custom builds, we'll handle whatever data is required for the engagement. Specific data-handling terms go in the engagement contract. As a default: we delete project data 90 days after engagement close unless you ask us to keep it.

Your rights

You can request what we have on you, ask us to delete it, or correct it, at any time. Email contact@dominionlabs.dev. We'll respond within 30 days, usually faster.

Children

Dominion Labs services are not directed at children under 13. If we learn we've collected data from a child under 13, we delete it.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we'll bump the date at the top + note what changed in our changelog. We won't retroactively use data for purposes outside the original collection scope.

Contact

Email contact@dominionlabs.dev. We read every message.
