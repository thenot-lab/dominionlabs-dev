where it goes

Plain talk about money.

Where donations actually go, so you know what you're funding.

  • Tools stay free. The miniapps, the open-source CLIs, the GitHub repos — donations keep them free for everyone, including people who can't pay.
  • Pass it forward. Donations enable pro-bono engagements for non-profits, students, and small shops who can't afford the full rate.
  • Real costs. Hosting, domains, software licenses, hardware to test on. Boring but real.
  • Time to ship more. Less time chasing paid gigs, more time shipping open tools.
  • No salaries paid. If we ever scale to that point, we'll say so on this page, openly.
